The brief in one page

Purpose

Get more people playing, by making every local arena discoverable, bookable and alive.

Positioning

The home of local sport: where players find the game and venues fill their slots. Unlike directories, ARENA shows what's live now, runs the competition and vouches for the venue.

Attributes

Live · Competitive · Communal · Trusted · Local with global ambition · Effortless · Energetic but premium

Personality

The confident captain: organises the game, knows every court, keeps it fair, isn't loud about it.

The tension

Street-court energy versus a trusted, premium platform. Resolution: the mark is calm and engineered; motion, photography and one live accent carry the noise.

What the category taught us

Avoid: green pitch rectangles, balls, swooshes, neon lime on black, generic pins, triangle As, orange "live" icons, flags. Open territory: ultraviolet, bone, clay; the place itself (bowl, ring, court) as the idea.
