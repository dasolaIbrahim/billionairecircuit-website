Recommendation

Take D5 Pin in the A forward, with D6's Centre Light as a secondary symbol for live states and motion. If you want more noise and attitude for social, D2's Syne voice is a strong campaign face. It shouldn't be the logo as well.

Why D5: it's the only direction where the name, the place and the product moment (find, then it's on) are one drawing. Its extracted pin A is a strong, unusual app icon and passes every small-size check.

Why pair it with D6: the split centre circle animates beautifully as the live pulse, so the app has a motion signature without changing the logo.

Violet keeps the brand clear of every status colour and of the crowded green, orange and blue territories in the audit. Bone on violet only.

The reviewer's alternative hybrid, 'Arch & Centre Light' (D5's arch letters with round counters and no pin, plus D6 as the symbol), is cleaner but loses D5's best idea. It's available if you prefer restraint.

This is a recommendation, not a decision: the pick is yours.
