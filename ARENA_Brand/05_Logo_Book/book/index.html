Court Lines

ARENA drawn with the court's own geometry: the A is the trapezoid key, its crossbar the free-throw line; the E's middle arm floats free like a painted lane line.

Match Day

Poster-weight extended lowercase in Syne ExtraBold: loud like a match-day poster. Each a has its counter filled and a location pin carved into the optical centre of the bowl; both a's match, so the word reads clean and symmetrical around the pins.

Live Pin

A map pin whose head is a stadium seen from above (the racetrack shape geometry literally calls a 'stadium'), with a live light switched on at its shoulder: there's a game near you, and it's on now.

Convergence Crest

A tournament bracket (four entrants, two semis, one final) carved into a shield: everyone meets here, the path resolves to one winner, and the venue is verified.

Pin in the A

The first A's counter is a map pin standing on the A's crossbar (find); the last A's counter holds the live light (play). Find it, then it's on: one word, the whole product.

Centre Light

The centre circle seen from the floodlight gantry: two halves split by the halfway line, gathered around the spot where every game starts, and the spot is lit.

Pixel Scoreboard

The GhostRevenue pixel system cloned for ARENA: a square-pixel pin mascot lit like a stadium LED scoreboard, with six poses that are the app's own statuses (available, live, booked, closed, fully booked, ask).
