# ARENA brand platform (v1, locked for exploration)

Source of truth: ARENA BRD v1.0 (4 May 2025) + master brief §1. Nothing here adds product facts the BRD doesn't hold.

## The problem, in one breath
Good local courts and pitches exist, but players can't find them, can't see if they're free or busy, and book them
by phone call and guesswork. Owners can't reach players, fill empty slots, or run tournaments without spreadsheets.

## Purpose
**Get more people playing**, by making every local arena discoverable, bookable and alive.

## Positioning
For players and the venues that host them, ARENA is **the home of local sport**: the one place where players find
the game and venues fill their slots. Unlike directories and generic booking tools, ARENA shows what's *live right
now*, runs the competition (brackets, standings, scorelines), and vouches for the venue (Verified Arena).

## Audiences, one brand
| Audience | What they need to feel | Proof in product |
|---|---|---|
| Players | "There's a game near me, and it's on." | Map + radius, Live Now light, 2-tap booking, tournaments, Live Feed, stats |
| Arena owners | "This is serious infrastructure for my business." | Slot manager, KYC (NIN), Verified badge with invoice + QR, tournament tools, analytics |
| Admins (Umbron) | "Clean, governable, trustworthy." | Moderation, reconciliation, audit logs |

## Attributes (validated)
| Attribute | Means | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Live** | Real-time, lit, happening now | Frantic, flashing |
| **Competitive** | Fair contests, results that count | Aggressive, toxic |
| **Communal** | Gathering, crowd, the regulars | Cliquey |
| **Trusted** | Verified venues, clear prices, no surprises | Corporate, cold |
| **Local, global ambition** | Rooted in Lagos/Abuja street-court culture, built to world standard | Folkloric cliché, flag-waving |
| **Effortless** | Two taps from "where?" to "booked" | Bare, generic |
| **Energetic but premium** | Floodlit-night energy with editorial restraint | Neon-on-black, gradient soup |

Validation note: the BRD's emphasis on verification, KYC, fee transparency and owner revenue makes **Trusted** a
first-class attribute, not a nice-to-have. It's the counterweight that stops the energy tipping into "tacky".

## Personality
**The confident captain**: organises the game, knows every court, keeps it fair, and isn't loud about it.
Bold, warm, fair, quick.

## Voice
Short, verb-first, specific (distances, times, scores). Straight from the product:
- Players: *Discover Courts Near You · Join Exciting Tournaments · Share Your Live Action*
- Owners: *Grow Your Venue's Bookings · Host and Manage Tournaments · Engage and Monetize Fans*
Tone shifts: hype for match day, plain and exact for money and bookings, never jokey about payments or trust.

## The design tension and how the identity resolves it
**Street-court energy ↔ trusted premium platform.** Resolution: *energy lives in motion, photography and one live
accent; trust lives in the mark's geometry, a restrained palette and confident typography.* The logo is calm and
engineered. The world around it (photos, pulse, scoreboard type) carries the noise.

## Brand architecture
- **ARENA** (masterbrand, player app)
- **ARENA for Venues** (merchant app / business sub-brand)
- Endorsed: **ARENA by DAC Sports** (corporate, investor, partner contexts only; ARENA leads)
- Programmes (later): ARENA Cup (tournaments), ARENA Juniors (academy/grassroots sub-system)

## Semantic territories for marks
Arena / bowl (aerial) · court and pitch lines · centre circle · location pin · live signal / floodlight ·
bracket / convergence · gathering crowd / ring · badge / crest of verification · motion and play.
