# Word-combination matrix

Method (from the logo-method research): list words from the attributes and product moments → cross-combine pairs →
keep only pairs that collapse into **one** picture → score → shortlist 12 → assign to directions.

## Scoring
S = simple (reads at 16 px) · O = ownable (not a category trope) · R = relevant (BRD moment) · Sc = scalable
(icon → floodlit banner) · St = story (rewards a second look). 1–5 each, max 25.
