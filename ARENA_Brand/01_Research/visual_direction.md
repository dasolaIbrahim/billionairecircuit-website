# ARENA — Visual Direction (research synthesis)

Source set: 38 seed pins (14 logo, 24 layout) + 96 "same-board" related pins = **134 images**.
Contact sheets: `01_Research/moodboards/` (`logo_moodboard.png`, `layout_moodboard.png`, `moodboard_marks_geometric.png`, `moodboard_layouts_editorial.png`, `moodboard_colour_type.png`). Manifest: `01_Research/moodboards/references_manifest.json`.

Retrieval notes: Pinterest's logged-out pin pages do not expose "more like this" pins, so related images come from the pin's own board (the board page is server-rendered). Pin 1088041591263514484 had no og:image in the plain HTML and was recovered through a headless browser. Nothing failed for good. Reference brand names are left out on purpose; marks are described by their construction only.

The short version: the previous attempt looked tacky because it spread several saturated colours, effects and type styles across everything. Every strong reference here does the opposite. It has **one geometric idea in the mark, one loud colour on a disciplined neutral field, and one big jump in type scale per layout**. Energy comes from scale and cropping. Decoration is not what makes it feel energetic.

## (b) Synthesis — logo construction moves

| Move | What we observed | Rule of thumb for ARENA |
|---|---|---|
| **One module** | Square i-dot = stem width (917678861593236106); spokes = stem (730498002102970994, 826480969153348803) | Pick one unit (stem width *s*). Dots, gaps, cuts and spokes are all multiples of *s* (0.5*s*, 1*s*). |
| **Heavy but open** | Best wordmarks run stems at about 18–24% of x-height, with counters still ≥ 1 stem wide | Bold or extra-bold, never black-black. Counters must survive 16 px. |
| **One custom letter** | Liquid s (527273068896946722), infinity O (1130473943985020461), crossbar-less A (1058064506221095857) | Modify at most one glyph family in ARENA (the As *or* the E). |
| **Negative space as meaning** | Arrow cut from a square (846606430010322283), star in a counter (984318062327904440), converging arrows (1100848702693950266) | Cut width 0.5–1*s*. The cut must mean something: pin, court line, direction or "live". |
| **Corners: pick one** | Either razor-sharp (square, arrow-cut) or fully round (soft caps, rounded tabs) | ARENA: sharp outer corners plus a single radius equal to *s*/2 on the inner cuts. Never mix three radii. |
| **Primitive geometry** | Circle, square, triangle, stripe, asterisk. No illustrative detail | Build from circle (ball / Live dot), rectangle (court) and radius arc (discovery). |
| **Stripes / parallels** | Equal stripe and gap (826480969148680452) | If lines are used, line = gap = *s*. Reads as court markings. |
| **Mono-first** | All grid sheets are 1-colour | The mark must work in 1 colour, 2-tone mono, and reversed before any brand colour is applied. |
| **Lock-up discipline** | Symbol height = cap or x-height plus ascender; descriptor about ¼ size with wide tracking | Symbol cap-height-locked. Descriptor tracked +8–12%, at ≤25% of wordmark size. |
| **Presentation ≠ logo** | Glass, LED, ribbed-glass and spotlight effects live in the *background* | Effects belong to photography and backgrounds, never to the mark itself. |

## (c) Layout system lessons

**Grid.** The best sets use one rigid frame repeated: corner micro-labels, a headline zone, an image zone and an arrow. Use a 4-column grid on 1080×1350 social, 12-column on banners, with 64–72 px outer margins (about 6%) and everything snapped to it.

**Type-scale contrast.** Premium comes from *the jump*. Headline-to-label ratios are 6:1 to 30:1 (116249234130890443, 907053181221712772). Use at most three sizes per layout: display, body, micro label.

**Weight at scale.** Very large type can be *light* (907053181221712772, 583568064258360772, 32791903531162815). Heavy condensed works for short punchy words of 1–3 lines. Never use heavy, wide and long copy together.

**Photo cropping.** Crop tight on human detail (594967800824955726) or wide with planned headroom (1139903355719319361). Avoid mid-distance "stock" framing. Never put text over faces or busy detail.

**Colour blocking.** Split frames at 50/50 (314196511521841565), bento tiles (32791903531162815), and container shapes holding text (404338872815656379, 1152780835885922964). Colour blocks are *flat*, never gradients.

**How much colour.** One owned colour per campaign (743516219776915032, 945122671825581052). Neutral fields (near-black, chalk) take about 80% of the area. The signal colour is a highlighter, not a background, for the "live" or key word only.

**How premium sports and editorial work avoids tackiness:**
- no outlines, bevels, glows, drop shadows or chrome on type
- no italic-slanted "speed" type
- no lightning bolts, flames or swooshes
- photography does the emotion while type stays calm and aligned
- generous empty space
- a consistent template that's boring on purpose, so the content can be loud

**Formats:**
- **App-store screenshots:** a headline in the top 30–35%, 2–3 lines, with one word in Live green. A cropped UI fragment (map, live card, bracket) bleeds off the bottom. Alternate chalk and near-black backgrounds. One message per screen.
- **Social posts:** the 4-corner label frame (sport / city / time / ↘). Use the photo-first variant or the type-only variant, nothing in between.
- **Banners:** headline left, photo right (split). Wordmark small, top-left. Avoid centred everything.
- **Splash screen:** mark at about 30% width on near-black with a soft top floodlight pool. The Live dot is the only colour, and it pulses once.

## (d) Recommended direction for ARENA: "Floodlit Editorial"

Street-court energy carried by **photography and scale**. Premium trust carried by **neutral fields, strict grid and restraint**. Sport's own graphic language (court lines, scoreboard numerals, the live dot, the map radius) replaces decoration.

### Layout rules
1. **One frame, always.** Micro labels in 4 corners (e.g. `PADEL` · `LEKKI` · `19:30` · `↘`) at 3% of the frame height, in tracked caps. Every format inherits it.
2. **One scale jump per layout.** Display ≥ 6× body. Maximum three type sizes.
3. **Photo or type, never both fighting.** Type goes on flat fields or in planned photographic headroom. Never over faces, crowds or busy backgrounds.
4. **Court-line geometry only.** Allowed graphic devices are hairline rules (court lines), the radius arc (discovery), the Live dot, the bracket line, and corner notches. Nothing else.
5. **Flat colour blocks, sharp edges.** Split 50/50 or bento. Use a single corner radius (12 px in UI, *s*/2 in brand) where rounding is used.
6. **Numbers are heroes.** Scorelines, distance ("2.1 km") and times get display size in tabular figures. Words get support size.
7. **Crop with intent.** Either tight detail (hands, ball, boots, net) or wide floodlit scene with top-third headroom. No mid-shot stock.
8. **Left-aligned by default.** Centred layouts only for splash and one-word statements.

### Colour restraint
| Token | Hex | Role | Share of area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink (warm near-black) | `#0E100F` | Primary dark field, text | ~45% |
| Chalk (court white) | `#F3F1EA` | Primary light field, text on dark | ~35% |
| Pitch (deep floodlit green) | `#0F3B2B` | Brand field, duotone shadows, type on Live | ~12% |
| Live (signal green) | `#22E27A` | **Only** live state, primary CTA, one highlighted word | ≤5% |
| Laterite (clay red-orange, used sparingly) | `#D2552B` | Tournaments / finals / heat; never with Live in the same frame | ≤3% |
| Concrete (grey) | `#8B8F8A` | Secondary text, hairlines | as needed |

- Live green is a **signal, not a brand wash**. If a screen has more than one Live-green element that isn't actually live or the primary action, remove one.
- Pair tonally: Pitch text on Live fields, Chalk text on Pitch fields. Never white on neon.
- No gradients in brand assets. The only "gradient" allowed is the photographic floodlight falloff.
- The Nigerian flag green (`#008751`) sits between Pitch and Live. Nod to it, but don't make the brand a flag.

### Type pairing (SIL OFL / Google Fonts only)
- **Display:** *Archivo* (variable: width 62–125, weight 100–900). Use the condensed widths at Bold–ExtraBold for posters and scorelines, and semi-expanded Medium for the wordmark starting point and short headlines. One family gives both street energy (condensed) and premium calm (wide).
- **Text and UI:** *Inter* (or *Inter Tight* for headings), using tabular figures (`tnum`) for every number in the app.
- **Data / scoreboard accent:** *JetBrains Mono* or *IBM Plex Mono* for match clocks, times and coordinates, used sparingly.
- *Optional* editorial accent: *Instrument Serif* italic for one word in long-form editorial (pin 196751077467776456 style). It is never used in the product UI.
- Alternatives to test: *Familjen Grotesk* (display), *Big Shoulders Display* (stadium-signage condensed, for wayfinding-style numerals only).
- Rules: at most two families on any one asset. Sentence case or lowercase for headlines, caps only for micro labels (tracked +8–12%). No faux-italic or skewed "speed" type.

### Photo grading
- **Golden hour (day):** warm low sun, long shadows on concrete and laterite, lifted blacks slightly warm (`#1A1510`-ish), skin tones protected and rich. Dust and haze welcome. Slight grain.
- **Floodlit night:** a cool-neutral pool of light on the court, crushed surround falling to Ink, a single practical light source visible (floodlight mast, phone torch). Shadows tinted toward Pitch green.
- **Subjects:** real Nigerian and African local play — 5-a-side on concrete and astro, outdoor basketball hoops, padel boxes, school fields, spectators on walls and kerbs, phones up filming. Authentic kit, not branded stock. Mixed genders and ages.
- **Treatment:** duotone (Ink→Chalk with Pitch shadows) for user-generated feed content in marketing. Keep LED dot-matrix and ribbed-glass overlays as *occasional* textures.
- **Never:** HDR, teal-orange blockbuster grading, AI-generated imagery, motion-blur effects added in post.

### Mark direction (in spirit)
- Build from *s* (stem) as the module.
- Explore: (1) ARENA wordmark with a crossbar-less or court-line A; (2) a symbol combining the radius arc and the Live dot (a "pin + pulse"); (3) a top-down court rectangle with one directional cut. The Live dot is the mark's one colour moment.
- Must pass: mono, 16 px favicon, embroidered on kit, stencilled on a court wall.

## (e) Avoid list (anti-tacky)
- Neon lime/green as a background wash, or neon on black as the default look
- Gradients on the logo or brand fields; glassmorphism; chrome or metal on the mark
- Outlined, beveled, glowing, drop-shadowed or 3D-extruded type
- Skewed or italic "speed" type, swooshes, lightning bolts, flames, speed lines
- More than one accent colour in a frame; a different colour per slide
- Starbursts, circular rotating text badges, halftone dot clusters, brush squiggles, stickers, emoji as decoration
- Heavy condensed caps over busy photographs
- Centring everything; more than three type sizes; more than two families
- Stock photography of generic gyms or non-local athletes; AI imagery; VR/neon futurism
- Mascots or literal ball illustrations in the mark; shield crests that mimic football clubs
- Using Live green for anything that isn't live or the primary action
- Flag-colour stripes as decoration

## Product conventions to respect

### Map discovery UI
- **Map is context, list is decision.** Use a bottom sheet with three snap points (peek / half / full) over the map. The peek state shows one hero venue card ("2.1 km · 6 min · Padel · Live now").
- **Calm basemap:** a custom desaturated style (Chalk land, Concrete roads, Ink labels; dark variant at night). Brand colour appears only in pins.
- **Pins:** one pin shape. State is shown by fill (Chalk = available, Pitch = booked, Live green dot plus a soft pulse ring = in use). Cluster at low zoom with a count. Selected pin scales by about 1.3× and its card syncs.
- **Radius:** a draggable radius ring with hairline stroke and 6–8% fill, labelled with distance on the ring (1 / 3 / 5 / 10 km chips). Distance and travel time always use tabular figures.
- **Filters** are pill chips in one row (sport, time, price, Verified). Avoid modal filter sheets for primary filters.
- **Accessibility:** "Live" must never rely on colour alone. Pair the dot with the word LIVE and the pulse, and meet WCAG contrast on map labels.

### Scoreboard and bracket design
- Scoreboards follow broadcast conventions. Team or player names are left-aligned in caps micro-label size, scores are right-aligned in large tabular numerals, and the clock or period is in mono. The winner's row is Chalk while the loser is dimmed to Concrete (contrast, not colour, signals the winner).
- The live score gets the Live dot and the word LIVE. Final scores get "FT". Upcoming fixtures show time instead of score.
- **Brackets:** horizontal scroll by round with sticky round headers (R16 · QF · SF · F). Connectors are 1 px hairlines (court lines) that turn Chalk/Pitch along the winner's path. Each match is a compact two-row card. On mobile, use a "focus my team" path view instead of the full tree.
- **League tables:** zebra-free rows with hairline dividers, tabular figures, and the user's team highlighted with a Pitch tint. Form guide as 5 small W/D/L squares in Chalk, Concrete and Laterite (not traffic-light colours).

### Stadium wayfinding conventions
- Stadium signage uses huge high-contrast numerals and letters (gates, blocks, courts), a reduced pictogram set, consistent arrows, and colour-coding by zone, all readable at distance and speed.
- **ARENA application:**
  - court numbers as giant condensed numerals on venue signage and in-app ("COURT 3")
  - a pictogram set for sports drawn on the stem module *s*
  - arrows from the mark's cut angle
  - zone colour limited to the existing palette
- Physical signage for "Verified Arena" venues (door decal, court-side board) should read like transport signage: Ink plate, Chalk numerals, Live dot when the court is in use. This connects the app's "Live Now" to the real court.
